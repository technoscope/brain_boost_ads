1. Introduction

Welcome to Brain Boost (“the Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). Brain Boost is a collection of seven puzzle games designed to challenge and sharpen your mind through hundreds of handcrafted levels across beautifully themed worlds.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use Brain Boost, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it.

By downloading or playing Brain Boost, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Brain Boost does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

The following non-personal data is stored locally on your device only:

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

Brain Boost displays rewarded video advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Watching a rewarded ad is entirely voluntary — you choose to watch an ad to earn an extra life.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • IP address (used to determine approximate geographic location)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, view duration)
  • Coarse location (country or region level, based on IP address)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under Google’s own privacy policy. Brain Boost does not receive, store, or have access to this data.

Advertising ID: Android users can reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Settings → Google → Ads on their device.

3.2 Google Play In-App Review

Brain Boost uses the Google Play In-App Review API to prompt players to rate the game on the Google Play Store. This flow is handled entirely by the Google Play Store app on your device. We do not receive your rating or any personal information through this mechanism.

5. Children’s Privacy

Brain Boost is a puzzle game suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about ads, you can disable personalized advertising via Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on your child’s device.

6. Data Security

All game progress is stored locally on your device and protected by your device’s own security mechanisms. We do not operate servers that store your personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored data is removed when you:

  • Uninstall Brain Boost (Android deletes all app data automatically).
  • Clear app data via Settings → Apps → Brain Boost → Storage → Clear Data.

There is no account to delete and no server-side data associated with you.

8. Your Rights

  • Deletion: Uninstall the app or clear app data to remove all locally stored information.
  • Advertising opt-out: Opt out of personalized ads via your device’s Google Ads settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of Brain Boost after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
